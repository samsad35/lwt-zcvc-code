Method Overview: Speaker-Covariance Monge Transport

Given self-supervised frame representations x ∈ ℝ1024 from layer 6 of WavLM-Large, Boosted LWT decomposes speech into K=10 soft phonetic clusters and applies the closed-form Bures-Wasserstein optimal transport map:

Tk(x) = μY,k + (x - μX,k) Ak,   with   Ak = ΣX,k-1/2 (ΣX,k1/2 ΣY,k(α) ΣX,k1/2)1/2 ΣX,k-1/2

The target covariance is boosted via universal background decomposition: ΣY,k(α) = Σshared,k + α (ΣY,k - Σshared,k). Setting α = 1.5 maximizes target speaker timbre without altering linguistic content (Wav2Vec2 CER = 0.71%, ECAPA similarity = 0.749).
